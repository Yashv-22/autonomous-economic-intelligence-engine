# 1. PROJECT CONTEXT

The project is an ambitious AI system intended to investigate and eventually solve problems emerging from the transformation of organizational operating models in the AI era.

The fundamental hypothesis is that widespread AI adoption is changing the speed, capacity, workflows, responsibilities, dependencies, and decision-making processes of different organizational functions.

This can potentially create new forms of organizational misalignment.

For example:

* One department may become dramatically more productive because of AI.
* Another department may still operate using legacy processes.
* A third department may become a bottleneck.
* Different teams may adopt AI at different rates.
* AI may increase the amount of work produced upstream without increasing downstream processing capacity.
* Existing workflows may become obsolete.
* Human-to-human coordination may become human-to-AI or AI-to-AI coordination.
* Existing organizational structures may no longer be optimal.
* New bottlenecks, dependencies, duplication, information gaps, approval queues, and decision delays may emerge.

The above is only an initial hypothesis.

**Do not assume that it is correct.**

The system must eventually be capable of researching, validating, challenging, refining, and discovering such hypotheses rather than merely confirming them.

The long-term vision is an AI-native system that can:

1. Research the Internet and relevant information sources.
2. Analyze research papers, reports, case studies, articles, and other documents.
3. Build a structured and continuously evolving knowledge base.
4. Understand organizational operating models and workflows.
5. Identify recurring problems caused or amplified by AI adoption.
6. Generate and rank hypotheses about organizational problems.
7. Investigate whether those problems are real and significant.
8. Identify gaps in existing solutions.
9. Discover potentially valuable automation opportunities.
10. Design AI-agent, SaaS, workflow, or operating-model interventions.
11. Eventually prototype and build those solutions.
12. Evaluate the solutions experimentally.
13. Learn from the results.
14. Improve its own software and capabilities under controlled conditions.

However, **all of the above is the long-term vision**.

Your current task is ONLY to design the architecture and implementation plan required to eventually achieve this vision.
