## 2. Canonical Open Questions Catalog

### Category A: Empirical & Operational Questions

#### Q-001: Domain-Specific Straight-Through Routing Limits ($\alpha_{\text{limit}}$) & Falsification Criteria
*   **The Question:** What is the maximum achievable straight-through automation rate ($\alpha$) across varying complexity domains (IT service desk vs. loan underwriting vs. clinical triage) before error rates exceed enterprise risk tolerance?
*   **Falsification Test:** If empirical tests show $\alpha \le 0.40$ in complex domains, what is the alternative economic driver (e.g., cycle-time compression, error reduction, rework elimination)?
*   **Resolution Method:** Conduct Phase 1–2 trials on 1,000 historical enterprise transaction logs.

#### Q-002: Human Reviewer Fatigue & Cognitive Degradation Function
*   **The Question:** At what transaction volume and shift length does Tier III (Human-in-the-Loop) oversight degrade into ceremonial rubber-stamping?
*   **Resolution Method:** Controlled empirical study measuring reviewer inspection times and error catch rates over extended supervisory sessions.

#### Q-003: Empirical Verification of External Survey Metrics
*   **The Question:** Do headline survey figures (88% GenAI adoption, <20% EBITDA return, 84% un-redesigned jobs) replicate when measured against audited enterprise financial records rather than executive sentiment surveys?
*   **Resolution Method:** Systematic literature extraction with primary-source verification in Phase 1.

### Category B: Technical & Architectural Questions

#### Q-004: Knowledge Graph vs. Relational Vector RAG Precision-Cost Boundary
*   **The Question:** Does a dedicated Neo4j Knowledge Graph provide statistically significant accuracy improvements over relational recursive CTEs + vector embeddings in multi-hop contradiction detection?
*   **Resolution Method:** Phase 2 Graph Evaluation Benchmark comparing precision, recall, and query latency across 5,000 test triples.

#### Q-005: Model Context Protocol (MCP) Scalability under Distributed Multi-Agent Load
*   **The Question:** What is the RPC latency overhead of stateless MCP 2026 servers under high-concurrency tool execution loads?
*   **Resolution Method:** Stress-testing MCP client-server clusters under simulated multi-agent research workloads.
